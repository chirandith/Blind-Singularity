Chapter 01 Pt.2 - The Elimination Engine

Soft. That was all he could think.

Seconds turned into minutes as they kept embracing each other. That night, they filled each other’s void which was formed by the lack of warmth in their lives.

It was almost 1am when they decided to head back. They held onto each other’s hands and with the help of Mira’s phone, they navigated through the trees.

When they reached the public road, they were met with a rather confusing view. It was midnight and all the houses nearby had gone to darkness with only a few street lamps lighting up parts of the road. And the road in front of them was scattered with hundreds of—

Fliers? Are these from the new year ceremonies? What a waste.

Visper picked one up near the sidewalk. It was about a “challenge for couples” due today, January first. Puzzled by the situation, he looked around. Not a single vehicle drove past and the city square was dead silent. He took another look at the flier and was surprised.

[ Prize for completion: ¥1,000,000,000 ]

O-One billion?? It was his first time even seeing a number that huge in front of his eyes. This can feed generations.

Delighted by the numbers, he did not even consider the thought of it being fake. With him having nothing but his life to lose, he thought to try it out. He again read the flier thoroughly and saw a map for the location and most importantly the part which said “for couples.”

He gave an awkward smile towards Mira but she already knew what he wanted. She said teasingly “I guess you have to find someone to fill the couple slot.”

She tried to walk away pretending to not care, only to get dragged into another longing contact with their lips. Even though this was Visper’s first time experiencing any kind of romance, he was not the slow burn type of person.

With the challenge due at 2pm and having nothing else to do, they considered resting at Mira’s apartment. They took a shortcut through the alleys and after around twenty minutes, they were there. It was a small but warm rental apartment. In the apartment was a kitchen, and a living space in one room and a separate room for the bedroom.

After making themselves comfortable, they both slept till the alarm sounded at 11am. It was undoubtedly the best rest Visper had in years. After cleaning themselves and having some toasts for breakfast they decided to head out.

The place they were to come to was almost two miles away from Mira’s apartment. Being tired from the roller-coaster of events last night, they were not in the mood to walk the whole way through, even though Visper preferred to spend as much as possible with Mira.

They took a cheap taxi to a town near that location and had to walk the rest as the place had no roads reaching to it.

It was 1:57pm when they came in front of the desired place. It was a two story warehouse, closed all around. Both reached to the front and knocked on the front door. Nothing happened.

Uh… Was this a joke?

He knocked again.

A few seconds later, the door opened. A man stood behind the door. He was dressed in a formal suit with a white mask that covered his whole head, not even leaving a strand of hair to be seen. The mask had a symbol of a sun painted in black on top, where the forehead would be.

Visper looked past him. Hundreds and hundreds of people stood in the middle of the warehouse. The warehouse was dimly lit with some spotlights targeting only the middle, leaving most of the warehouse in darkness. There was a huge digital clock in the far back, mounted to the wall, indicating 1.58pm.

“Please come in,” the man spoke.

Visper sighed and took a step in only to get dragged back. He looked behind to see Mira frozen. He gave a smile and comforted her.

“Don’t worry. If it’s too much trouble we’ll just leave.”

Mira nodded faintly and both took a step into the warehouse. It was 1.59pm. Both slowly walked closer to the middle. It was surprisingly quiet for how many people there were.

There’s at least two thousand people here. Are we really supposed to compete against all of them?

He thought as he looked around confusingly. Mira held his hand and never dared to take a step even a little farther than him. Seconds passed, and when the clock hit 2pm, both were standing in the spotlight.

After a few seconds of pure silence,

Clunk!

All the lights went out. Quietness broke as they all started to worry in panic. Even before they could register what was happening—

“Welcome to The Elimination Engine.” The speakers around the corners of the warehouse screamed with the voice of a male.

E-Elimination Engine?

Everyone was left confused. After another few seconds of silence whispers filled with panic, the sounds of the speakers continued.

“Well then

Let the challenge begin!”

BZZZZT–

In less than a second, a bright white light flashed everyone, making them cover their eyes with their hands. A few seconds later, after feeling nothing else was happening, Visper slowly opened his eyes.

He was standing in a space filled with bright white. Everyone had disappeared from his view and it was just him in this endless white void. Even with such odd events, Visper never panicked. He observed everything keenly to see “the purpose” of this situation. He bent to see if he could touch the floor and suddenly popped out in front of him.

It was a translucent, gray-ish rectangular plain floating in front of him. He stood up to clearly look at it, when he saw that there was words written on it.

[ The Elimination Engine ]

[ Would you like to proceed? ]

[ Yes ] [ No ]

Is this… am I inside a simulation?

Without much thought, he clicked “Yes”. It gave a pop sound and the words in front of him vanished, revealing another set of words.

[ The Elimination Engine ]

[ Select a system ]

System? What the hell is this?

The text on the floating window gave him seven options to choose. Kinetic, Radiation, Volt, Thermal, Gravity, Hydra and Burst. Under all was an 8th option as ‘Singularity’ but it was not selectable.

What am I supposed to do with no information about each? Singularity does sound cool but I can’t select it.

He pondered for over ten minutes and finally chose to go with ‘Kinetic’. Don’t make me regret this. He thought inwardly.

Pop!

The text vanished and gave another set of words.

[ Good Luck! ]

Silence. The floating window vanished. It was him and the void yet again. Almost a minute passed by. He looked in all directions to be ready for whatever to come.

KYAA—

Suddenly, a throbbing pain hit his head. He looked around in confusion and noticed his view starting to become blurry and shaky as if he was nauseous. After a few seconds of trying to stay focused, he lost his consciousness.
