Chapter 01 - Till Death Do Us Apart

“What the..”

He could do nothing but make assumptions of what had happened a few moments ago.

He, Visper, was a seventeen year old who was left behind on the streets by his parents when he was three just because he was not enough of a good model for their business advertisement. Visper lived most of his life with his grandmother until she passed away of old age two years ago.

He tried to open his eyes but a light as bright as a sun flashed him making him blind for a few moments. Eventually, he was greeted with a rather unfamiliar view.

The sun?

It was a plain field covered with grass that was almost his knees’ height. He was standing on the edge of a cliff. To his forward, almost a mile away, was a mountain range and a view of an ocean covered in his behind. He looked around hesitatingly and saw more people waking up in the grass field.

“What is happening?” His instincts went blank as he tried to recall his memories, convinced that he was in a dream.

I was just with Mira?

We applied to that game and…

***

It was the New Year’s Eve for the year five hundred, the so-called ‘breakpoint year’ of humanity. Even though it was thought to be five hundred years old, the world was suspiciously modern. They had skyscrapers and fully advanced vehicles and mobile devices, yet no one questioned how those came to be in such a limited time.

People of the Meridian City gathered around the city square in the hopes of celebrating a once in a lifetime moment. Everyone cheered and laughed. As for Visper…

He took everything in on a distant bench. It was the second year of him spending New Year’s Eve alone but he had already accepted his fate. For a seventeen year old, he was more mature than most adults, maybe with his experiences of rejection from society.

“Hey!” A voice beside him.

Visper turned to see a silhouette of a woman with a scarf pulled tight around her.

***

Visper came back to his senses as he organized his memories together.

“Don’t tell me this is what they meant by a ‘game’” He whispered to himself as he looked around.

There were about two hundred people gathered around in a circle. They all looked around hesitatingly and puzzled with the unfamiliar gaze.

There were over a thousand people in the lobby, what happened to the others?

And I don’t see Mira either.

Suddenly a voice rang through the clouds.
