Chapter 01 Pt.1 - Warmth in Life

“What the..”

He could do nothing but make assumptions of what had happened a few moments ago.

He, Visper, was a seventeen year old who was left behind on the streets by his parents when he was three just because he was not good enough of a model for their business advertisement. Visper lived most of his life with his grandmother until she passed away of old age two years ago.

He tried to open his eyes but a light as bright as a sun flashed him making him blind for a few moments. Eventually, he was greeted with a rather unfamiliar view.

The sun?

It was a plain field covered with grass that was almost his knees’ height. He was standing on the edge of a cliff. In front of him, almost a mile away, was a mountain range and a view of an ocean covered in his behind. He looked around hesitatingly and saw more people waking up in the grass field.

“What is happening?” His instincts went blank as he tried to recall his memories, convinced that he was in a dream.

I was just with Mira?

We applied to that game and…

He slowly pieced his memories together.

***

It was the New Year’s Eve for the year five hundred, the so-called ‘breakpoint year’ of humanity. Even though it was thought to be five hundred years old, the world was suspiciously modern. They had skyscrapers and fully advanced vehicles and mobile devices, yet no one questioned how those came to be in such a limited time.

People of the Meridian City gathered around the city square in the hopes of celebrating a once in a lifetime moment. Everyone cheered and laughed. As for Visper…

He took everything in on a distant bench. It was the second year of him spending New Year’s Eve alone but he had already accepted his fate. For a seventeen year old, he was mentally more mature than most adults, maybe with his experiences of rejection from society.

He made himself busy by trying to figure out the concept of a ‘breakpoint year.’

Now that I think about it… How did we even get here in five hundred years? Thirty years to go from dial phones to smartphones—

The thought itched, twisting deeper.

but only four hundr—

“Hey!” A voice beside him.

Visper turned to see a silhouette of a woman with a scarf pulled tight around her.

“Your gloominess is kinda ruining the city’s view.” She continued while bending to sit on the bench.

Visper was left even more confused. “Oh– I’ll move.” He replied with a common phrase that he has to blurt out often while trying to survive in the city.

“Pfft— bad day? I was only joking.”

It was a rare view on the Visper’s part because the last time he had someone laugh beside him was his grandmother, more than two years ago.

“I’m Mira. Nice to meet you.” She greeted him with the biggest grin.

“Visper.” He cut the greetings short not because he is not much of a talker, but because he was truly speechless.

The two talked for almost thirty minutes. She asked questions, he answered, continued with silence for a few seconds and repeated the same process.

In those thirty minutes, Visper had learned a lot about her. Like where she is from, what she does for work, her family background and most importantly the fact that she was alone just like him.

“It’s almost The New Year. Wanna grab something to eat? Maybe Ramen?” She invited him and just as the conversation went, he could do nothing but go along with her.

The restaurant was crowded, filled with joy and laughter. Mira walked into an empty booth and waved at Visper to follow her. Both sat opposite to each other and she took the lead to order their food.

“Are you sure we can pay for these?” Visper asked hesitatingly.

“I saved up a lot in these past few months.” She replied with a smile, as if tomorrow did not matter. Visper on the other hand was on the fence of believing it was a dream all along.

The food came later than usual with the New Year’s rush. Mira dug in without hesitation, grinning with satisfaction, while Visper ate more carefully.

They had spent almost two hours in the restaurant. Throughout the whole time, she kept talking, asking about small things such as his favourite food, what he likes to do often and his background. Nothing too deep but Visper felt a sense of relief just by putting everything out.

When they got out of the restaurant there was less than fifteen minutes till the New Year.

“Follow me. There’s a place I need to show you.” Mira grabbed his hand and ran.

Confused by the suddenness, Visper blurted out the question “Where?”, but seeing the smile on her face made all his worries blank.

They left towards a mountainous area, leaving the city behind. The further they walked, the quieter it became. Mira’s smartphone was the only source of light as the forest swallowed them, leaving them in full darkness.

After a few minutes of walking, they broke through the trees and were at the top of a hill.

Visper was left speechless.

Below them, the city glittered with lights. Fireworks burst into colours and the city became louder as the countdown reduced. For Visper, it was quite overwhelming.

Mira stood beside him with hands in her pockets. “Nice huh? No crowds. No noise. Just us and the view.”

“...Yeah,” Visper agreed.

For a time, neither of them spoke. The world below pulsed with life, but up there, it was only silence.

His head was filled with thoughts such as what will happen after this? Will we meet again? Or is she doing all of these out of pity?

He glanced at her and saw she was trying to say something. Her lips moved, but didn’t say a word. Instead, she stepped closer.

The distant city began to roar with the countdown.

“Ten… Nine… Eight…”

Visper’s pulse raced. “M-Mira—”

“Seven… Six… Five…”

She stood right in front of him. He could see the reflection of fireworks sparking in her eyes.

“Four… Three… Two…”

Her hand touched his cheek, warm against the cold night.

“One—”

Fireworks exploded, painting the sky with colours. And before Visper could process anything—

Her lips were on his.
